Key Concepts Cheat Sheet — Click to expand
EVM Formulas
CPI=EV/AC — <1 over budget
SPI=EV/PV — <1 behind schedule
EAC=BAC/CPI (typical)
EAC=AC+(BAC-EV) (atypical variance)
ETC=EAC−AC
VAC=BAC−EAC
TCPI=(BAC−EV)/(BAC−AC)
Schedule Compression
Crashing=Add resources to critical path. Costs increase. No extra risk.
Fast Tracking=Overlap sequential activities. Risk increases. No extra cost.
Rule: No resources → Fast Track. Have budget → Crash.
Stakeholder Grid
High Power + High Interest=Manage Closely
High Power + Low Interest=Keep Satisfied
Low Power + High Interest=Keep Informed
Low Power + Low Interest=Monitor
Salience Model: Power + Legitimacy + Urgency
Conflict Resolution
Collaborating=Win-Win → Most durable
Compromising=Lose-Lose → Moderate
Smoothing=Temporary fix → Short-term
Forcing=Win-Lose → Least preferred
Withdrawing=No resolution
Risk Response
Threats: Avoid, Transfer, Mitigate, Accept
Opportunities: Exploit, Share, Enhance, Accept
Residual risk=after response applied
Secondary risk=created BY the response
Workaround=unplanned, for unknowns
Contract Types
FFP=Fixed Price — seller high risk
CPFF=Cost+ Fixed Fee — buyer high risk
T&M=Time & Material — mixed risk
CPIF=Cost+ Incentive — share savings
FPIF=Fixed Price + Incentive
Tuckman's Stages
1. Forming→Directing
2. Storming→Coaching
3. Norming→Supporting
4. Performing→Delegating
5. Adjourning→Recognizing & closing
Agile / Scrum Roles
Product Owner=backlog owner, priorities, ROI
Scrum Master=coach, removes impediments, servant-leader
Dev Team=self-organizing, cross-functional
Sprint=1–4 week time-box
DoD=team-defined 'done' criteria
Communication Channels
Formula: N×(N−1)/2
5 people=10 channels
10 people=45 channels
20 people=190 channels
Push=send to recipients
Pull=retrieve on demand
Interactive=real-time two-way
Quality Tools (7 Basic)
Control Chart=detect variation (UCL/LCL)
Pareto=80/20 rule, prioritize defects
Ishikawa=root cause (fishbone)
Scatter=correlation between variables
Histogram=frequency distribution
Run Chart=trends over time
Kanban Principles
WIP Limits=reduce bottlenecks, improve flow
Pull System=team pulls work when ready
No sprints—continuous delivery
Cycle time=key metric
CFD=Cumulative Flow Diagram tracks flow
Lead time=request to delivery
Change Control Process
1. Evaluate impact on all baselines
2. Submit to CCB
3. CCB approves or rejects (document both)
4. Update affected plans/baselines
5. Implement only after approval
Gold plating=unauthorized feature adds
Motivation Theories
Maslow: Physiological→Safety→Social→Esteem→Self-actualization
Herzberg: Motivators=achievement, recognition; Hygiene=salary, conditions
Theory X: people avoid work
Theory Y: people self-motivate
McClelland: nAch, nAff, nPow
Organizational Structures
Functional: PM=low authority
Weak Matrix: PM=limited authority
Balanced Matrix: PM=shared authority
Strong Matrix: PM=high authority
Projectized: PM=full authority
PMO types: Supportive/Controlling/Directive
Cost of Quality
Conformance Costs:
Prevention (training, design reviews)
Appraisal (inspection, testing)
Non-Conformance Costs:
Internal failure (rework, scrap)
External failure (warranty, complaints)
Prevention $$$ saves failure $$$$$
Float / Critical Path
Total Float=LS−ES or LF−EF
Free Float=can delay without affecting successor's ES
Critical Path=longest path, zero float
Forward pass=calculate ES/EF
Backward pass=calculate LS/LF
Float=0 on critical path
